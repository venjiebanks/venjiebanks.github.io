Privacy Policy for Verba

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Verba: Gospel & Prayer is developed by Venjie Banks. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app and visit this privacy-policy page.

1. Information stored on your device

Verba works without an account. Your collected Gospel readings, favorites, reading history and streak progress, onboarding status, reading preferences such as text size, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. Downloaded readings and calendar information may also be cached locally so they can be used again.

When recovering a damaged collection or favorites value, the app may keep a local recovery copy of the original value. The app does not upload your collection, favorites, or preferences to a developer-operated database, and it does not provide its own cross-device cloud sync. Your device or browser may separately back up or restore app data according to its settings.

The current app does not require a name, email address, login credentials, or access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated crash-reporting service.

2. Requests to Bible and calendar services

Some readings and reflections are included in the app. When additional content is needed, the app can connect to external services:

  • NET Bible API (labs.bible.org) for Bible passage text.
  • Catholic Readings API (hosted on GitHub Pages) for dated Sunday Gospel references and celebration information.
  • Bible API (bible-api.com) for World English Bible passage text.
  • Other reading or archive functions may use Daily Lectio for devotional content or the Liturgical Calendar API for calendar titles.

These requests can include the passage reference or calendar date being requested. The receiving service and its hosting providers also receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, request time, and technical request headers. A request can therefore reveal which Bible passage or reading date you access. Verba does not attach your local collection, favorites, or contact details to these content requests.

These services operate independently and handle requests and logs under their own privacy practices. Their data retention and processing locations are controlled by those providers. The app's bundled reflections do not require a runtime AI service and are not sent to an AI provider for generation.

3. Daily reminders

If you allow notifications, Verba schedules reminders locally through your device's operating system using the times you select. The current reminder feature does not register you with a developer-operated push-notification server.

You can turn reminders off in Verba's Settings or change notification permission in your device settings. Notification text may appear on your lock screen, depending on your device's notification-preview settings.

4. Sharing and the clipboard

Sharing happens when you choose to share a Gospel reading or prayer. Verba passes the selected text, and any included date, title, reference, and translation label, to the device or browser share feature. The app or service you choose then handles that content under its own privacy practices. Verba does not choose recipients or read your contacts.

On the web, when sharing is unavailable or fails, the app may offer or use a clipboard fallback to copy the text. A manual copy option is also available when supported sharing or clipboard access is unavailable. Other apps or clipboard-sync features may have access to copied text according to your device settings. Verba does not read your clipboard contents.

5. Support messages

If you email verba.app2026@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot reported issues. Please do not send passwords or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

6. Retention and deleting local data

Your local collection, favorites, preferences, recovery copies, and cached content remain in app or browser storage until they are removed, replaced, or cleared. Some caches are refreshed or limited automatically.

  • iPhone or iPad: use your device settings to delete Verba and its app data. Offloading an app can retain its documents and data.
  • Android: use your device settings to clear Verba's app storage or uninstall the app.
  • Web: clear the browser's site data for the website where you use Verba.

Your operating system, browser, or backup provider may retain separate backup copies. Manage those copies through that provider's settings. Deleting Verba does not delete text you already shared with another app, messages you sent, clipboard history, or logs held independently by content providers.

We cannot remotely view, export, or erase your on-device collection. There is no Verba account to delete. For questions about information you sent directly to us, contact the email address below.

7. This website and external links

This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends normal web-request information, including your IP address, to GitHub and its hosting infrastructure. See GitHub's General Privacy Statement. This page does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, or cookies of its own.

Opening a support, developer, or other external link takes you to a separately operated page or service. Its own privacy practices apply.

8. Your choices, privacy requests, and security

You can control local app storage, notification permission, sharing, and device backups through the app and your device or browser settings. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or other privacy rights under applicable law. Contact us to make a request. These requests concern information we actually hold, such as support correspondence; local app data remains under your control, and independent providers handle their own records.

App data is stored using your device or browser's app-storage facilities. Local storage is not a secure vault, and no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect access to your device, browser profile, and backups.

9. Children's privacy

Verba is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support correspondence. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
